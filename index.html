About

I am an M.S. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, advised by Prof. Jongheon Jeong at the Trustworthy AI Lab.

My research focuses on prompt optimization and controllable generative modeling, particularly for large language models and video generative models. I am interested in developing efficient methods that enable generative models to adapt to diverse tasks and user intent.
